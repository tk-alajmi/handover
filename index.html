Fill in the sections below, add as many alert/rule entries as the shift needs, then generate the formatted handover text to paste into your team channel.

⚠️
DO NOT enter internal or identifying information

This form is for shift statistics and narrative summaries only. Do not type any of the following into any field:

  • IP addresses (internal or external) or subnet ranges
  • MAC addresses
  • Usernames, service account names, or email addresses
  • Hostnames / machine names / asset tags
  • Ticket numbers or customer names that identify the client

Describe activity generically instead — e.g. "SNMP scan from a local host" rather than the IP itself. Fields are scanned locally for obvious IP/MAC/email/username patterns as a safety net, and a "Remove flagged text" button lets you strip the matched text with one click — but the analyst is responsible for what's typed in. This scan runs entirely in your browser: no AI, no API calls, nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
